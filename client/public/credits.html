Environment

Park, terrain, furniture, vegetation and props: Poly Haven · CC0. Gazebo, vehicles, stairs, hospital equipment, computer equipment and interior props: Innerscene · CC0. Retained secondary furnishings: Kenney · CC0. Exact original-model pages are in the Poly Haven catalog and Innerscene catalog.

Ceiling fan: Ulan Cabanilla · Poly Haven · CC0. Original geometry and 1k textures packed in Blender; mount and rotor pivot aligned for runtime animation.

Campaign voices

All nine acts and authored character replies recorded locally with Kokoro-82M v1.0 (Apache 2.0) through Kokoro ONNX (MIT). Distinct stock voices, normalized recordings, and audio-driven mouth movement. The inference model is a local build tool; the game serves MP3s without a paid TTS service.
