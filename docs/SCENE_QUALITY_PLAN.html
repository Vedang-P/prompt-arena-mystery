PROMPT ARENA / QUALITY PLAN / 11 OCTOBER 2026

Finish each location as a coherent, believable place.

The current campaign has 14 playable environments, nine acts, 109 cinematic cues, 46 puzzles and two endings. This plan covers every reuse of each environment in gameplay, openings and closings. Act 0 is the visual reference; its locations still receive the same final checks.

Working rule: complete one location through asset selection, layout, detailing, lighting, cinematics, interaction and performance checks before moving to the next. Reuse the approved environment everywhere. A screenshot alone does not establish that a scene works.

1. Lock the common scene standard

  1. Art direction: believable contemporary Indian locations, restrained mystery atmosphere, realistic human proportions, warm practical lights against cooler night ambience. Keep dark teal, brass and Inter for interface elements. Avoid mixing realistic people with blocky principal characters.
  2. Free assets: acquire finished models with verified redistribution rights. Prefer Poly Haven CC0 and Microsoft Rocketbox MIT; use other original creator sources only after checking the individual license. Keep a source/license/modification record for each asset. No paid assets or homemade substitute hero models.
  3. Blender preparation: import source assets, inspect topology/materials, unify metres and axes, apply transforms, ground origins, measure floor/desk/seat surfaces, check normals and textures, make LODs and export optimized GLBs. Preserve original geometry and author attribution. Use Blender Python automation for repeatable checks and exports.
  4. Shared layout data: define furniture transforms, contact surfaces, character standing/seated marks, interaction anchors and cinematic camera marks once per location. Every scene consumes those values; no copied coordinate fixes. Replace floating yellow/blue rings with compact object-attached markers, group overlapping actions and use the same teal/brass controls throughout.
  5. Test harness: maintain a development-only gallery of all 14 environments and 62 distinct cinematic arrangements. It must use the actual game renderers, wait for drawing and animation settling, and avoid team progress writes. Keep before/after captures and a defect list.

3. Mandatory pass for each location

  1. Layout pass: floor and furniture contacts, scale, rotations, door/stair continuity, supported seated hips/feet, no floating props or intersections. Examine the actual animation cycle, not the bind pose.
  2. Art pass: compatible models, material maps, exposure, grounded shadows, believable density and clear composition. Remove conspicuous placeholders and accidental empty space; preserve deliberate walking/viewing space.
  3. Camera pass: wide, two-person and portrait views plus every opening, closing, replay and transition. Confirm feet remain in wide shots, faces remain unobstructed and dialogue controls stay inside the viewport. Check 1920×1080, 1366×768/1280×800, portrait and short landscape layouts.
  4. Interaction pass: discover every clue, open every puzzle, inspect artwork, consult the notebook, replay dialogue and use every exit. Markers follow the object surface and remain readable. Test keyboard, pointer and touch fallback controls.
  5. Audio pass: listen to all recorded lines for pronunciation, intelligibility and role suitability. Regenerate weak takes. Verify pause/resume, mute, skip, replay, subtitle timing, mouth movement and music ducking. Test blocked autoplay, a failed audio download and returning to the same room.
  6. Performance pass: profile an integrated-GPU classroom device; balanced target at least 30 FPS and desktop high target 60 FPS. Lazy-load rooms, use 1k/2k textures appropriately, limit active shadows and instanced background props, and check memory after repeated transitions. Optimize based on measured frame time.
  7. Evidence: save representative before/after images, all-shot captures, contact measurements, performance observations and remaining defects. Mark the location complete only when the checks pass.

Build a Vercel candidate, validate its exact version and asset hashes, inspect the candidate in a browser, then promote it. Require no known crashes, progression blockers, major intersections, unreadable clues or clipped controls. Keep a working rollback version. Do not label the entire game finished while any environment remains on the pending list.

Free speech implementation

Kokoro-82M has Apache-licensed weights. The current implementation generates stock-voice recordings locally through Kokoro ONNX and serves MP3s. Clients do not download or run the 325 MB model, and no TTS API subscription is required. The catalog currently covers 217 unique authored lines (22.5 minutes, 13.1 MiB total), fetched as needed. Newly authored dialogue must be added to that catalog and regenerated.
